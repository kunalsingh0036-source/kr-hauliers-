BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few useful answers.

Not sure which service fits your load? Send the route and cargo details, and we’ll work from there.

Where do you operate?

KR Hauliers serves routes across East Africa, with yards in Mombasa and Nairobi. Share your collection and delivery points so the team can confirm the route.

What can you move?

Our services cover steel and minerals, agricultural goods, FMCG and general regional freight, alongside clearing and forwarding support. Tell us about any special handling requirements.

How is a quote worked out?

The route, cargo type, weight, dimensions, handling requirements and timing all affect a quote. The team will confirm the details with you before you book.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Start with your name, email, collection point, destination and cargo type. Weight, quantity and preferred dates are helpful if available.

Does sending an enquiry book a truck?

No. An enquiry starts the conversation. Availability, documentation and booking terms need to be agreed with the team before transport is confirmed.
